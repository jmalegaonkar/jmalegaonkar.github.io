Jai Malegaonkar

Hello! I recently graduated from UC San Diego with a Master's in Computer Science and Engineering. I am currently a Research Intern at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, where I work on learning allocation mechanisms for heterogeneous resources with combinatorial bids.

My research interests lie at the intersection of embodied AI, human-AI interaction, multi-agent systems, and meta-reinforcement learning. In particular, I am interested in how AI agents can adapt to the people and other agents they regularly interact with by learning their behaviors, preferences, and strategies, allowing for more effective collaboration.

I am exploring PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
